CineLog Library Wall Redesign

Solving visual clutter by cleanly separating Movies, TV Shows, and Anime.

Concept 1 · Recommended

Categorized Shelves & Dedicated Sections

How it works: The library wall is naturally grouped into 3 distinct sections: 🎬 Feature Films (34), 📺 Television Series (12) with electric blue episode pills, and ⛩️ Anime & Animation (8) with sunset amber badges. Movies never intermingle with shows or anime.
Concept 2

Instant Top Segmented Vault

How it works: Large tactile pills at the top ([🎬 Movies] [📺 TV] [⛩️ Anime]) let you focus on 1 medium at a time with 100% full-height vertical space and zero clutter.
Concept 3

Unified Flow with Vibrant Badges & Toggle

How it works: Keeps a chronological timeline, but gives every poster an unmistakable, color-coded corner pill (Red Movie, Blue TV with progress bar, Orange Anime) and a top toggle for Unified vs Grouped.
